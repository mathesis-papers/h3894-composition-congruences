# Hanna's OEIS A389474 FORMULA (3): a modulo-nine proof and companion congruences

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

We prove Hanna's conjecture in OEIS A389474, FORMULA (3), and the companion conjectures in A389474, FORMULA (4), A389475, FORMULA (4.a), (4.b), and A389472, COMMENTS. For the first equation we establish A(x)^2 = x^2 + 2x^3 + 9Q(x), with Q in Z[[x]]. Integral coefficient constructions, Frobenius and binomial descent prove the six congruences. We also establish reversion identities and every even-power congruence modulo nine. If D is the A350432 series, every rational-seed solution of the A389472 equation is C_s=x+sD^2. Its product representation proves the stated convergence radius (sqrt(5)-1)/2 for D and every C_s with s nonzero.
